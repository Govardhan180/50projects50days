Our Story

Hotel Pravallika Veg & Non Veg began as a small family kitchen serving travelers and local families on Pedabayalu Main Road. Over the years, we have grown into a beloved dining destination known for generous portions and genuine Andhra hospitality.

We blend traditional recipes with fresh ingredients every day, ensuring every meal tastes like home. From hearty veg thalis to rich non-veg gravies, our chefs prepare each dish with care and signature spice blends.

Specialties

  • 🔥 Andhra Chicken Fry
  • 🍛 Dum Biryani (Veg & Non-Veg)
  • 🥘 Mutton and Seafood Curries
  • 🥬 Daily Veg Meals with seasonal dishes
  • 🍢 Evening snacks and starters
